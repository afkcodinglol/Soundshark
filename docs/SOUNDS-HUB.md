# Sounds Hub

Sounds Hub lets you discover community sounds, preview them, and download copies
into your local Library. You can also share audio you own or have permission to
share. Library files already on your PC remain available without a Hub account.

## Create an account

1. Click **Sounds Hub**, then **Create Account**.
2. Enter your email, a unique username of 3–24 letters, numbers or underscores,
   and a password of 6–12 characters.
3. Choose **Remember me for 30 days** if you want to stay signed in on this PC.
4. Click **Create Account**. A separate window asks for the code emailed to you.
5. Enter the code and select **Verify Code**. Check your spam folder if needed.

Three incorrect codes require restarting the sign-up process. Network failures
do not use an attempt. You can sign in using either your username or your email.
Remembered sessions are encrypted for your Windows user and expire 30 days after
sign-in; refreshing a session does not extend the deadline. Without remember-me,
you sign in again the next time you open the Hub.

## Reset your password

Click **Forgot Password** beside **Create Account**. Enter your username or email
in the recovery window. When the account is found, **Send Recovery Code** lights
up green. Click it to email a code to the account's address. The same window then
asks for that code, followed by **New Password** and **Confirm New Password**.

**Send Again** has a 60-second cooldown. Three incorrect recovery codes require
restarting. Recovery requests expire after 15 minutes. **Set New Password** lights
up green when both passwords match and contain 6–12 characters. The current
password cannot be reused; complete historic password reuse is not tracked.
After resetting, sign in with the new password.

Errors appear in red. Password fields show a warning while Caps Lock is on.
Never share verification codes with other people.

## Share, download and report

- **Refresh Sounds** reloads the current catalogue. Successful uploads and
  deletions refresh automatically while keeping the search and My uploads filter.
- **Upload Sound** prepares an MP3 copy and independently validates it on the
  service. Each sound is limited to 60 seconds and 10 MB. Only share audio you
  have permission to distribute.
- **Download To Library** saves a local copy without replacing an existing file.
- **My uploads** shows your contributions. You can remove your own uploads;
  removing a listing does not delete copies other people already downloaded.
- **Report Sound** records a private report. Three reports from different
  verified accounts hide the sound pending review. No email notification is sent.

The service currently limits accounts to five upload attempts per hour and 50
active uploads, with a shared storage limit. Reports are limited to 20 per day.
Hub sounds and uploader names are shared with other Hub users; passwords and
email addresses are not part of public catalogue listings.
