# Country flag assets

The PNG flags and `countries.json` are sourced from
`hampusborgos/country-flags` (including its historic pre-rendered PNG set).
The project states that the flag artwork is in the public domain and names the
files by ISO 3166 country or region code. Kosovo uses the commonly assigned
`XK` code; the constituent countries of the United Kingdom use their extended
codes.

Source: https://github.com/hampusborgos/country-flags
